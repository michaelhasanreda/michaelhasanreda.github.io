MICHAEL REDA

Tuppence in York

A children's picture book written by my wife, Debs, which I illustrated. We published it in 2024. All illustrations are pen and ink drawings with watercolour washes. Those who know York will recognise many scenes. Copies are for sale in Upside Down Designs and Topping and Company. You can also buy or borrow a copy from York Library.

About Me

I am an artist based in York. I work mostly in oil paint. I occasionally work in charcoal, pen and ink, and watercolour.
